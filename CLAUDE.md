# Operating notes for AI assistants working in this repository

## What this is
The live website of a licensed Ontario mortgage broker. Everything under `site/` is public the moment it reaches `main`.

## Locked baseline (2026-10-13)
Hamed has locked the site as it stands on branch `locked/v2-2026-10-13` (commit 89b2fc1). Do not change design, layout, text, images, the Start form, the Make scenario or the Zoho workflow unless Hamed asks for that specific change in the conversation. Routine exceptions he has already approved: drafting new Insights posts on a branch for his approval, and updating the Insights rate strip after a Bank of Canada decision (also via a branch and his approval). Never move or delete the locked branch.

## Rules
1. `main` is production. Push to a branch first, check the Vercel preview, then merge. Never claim something is live without opening the live address and checking.
2. Make the smallest change that solves the problem. Do not restructure or restyle pages that were not asked about.
3. Mortgage facts (numbers, rules, promises, fees) are published under Hamed's licence. Do not add or change one without his explicit sign-off. Unreviewed pages keep their `<p class="review">` label and `noindex`, and stay out of `site/sitemap.xml`.
4. Photos of Hamed must be his real photos. No generated or altered likeness.
5. The eight questions on the Start form are fixed. Do not change them here; their source is a separate document.
6. Do not touch DNS, the domain, or the Make scenario from this repository's work without Hamed's explicit approval.
7. Do not remove `site/google0acff6827b529d97.html` or the redirects in `vercel.json`.
8. Both languages change together: every page under `site/` has a twin under `site/fa/`.

## How to verify a change
- Open the preview address and the changed page in both languages.
- Check the browser console for errors and that internal links resolve.
- For the Start form, a test submission creates a real Lead in Zoho CRM. Use a name containing `[TEST-DELETE-OK]` and tell Hamed the record ID. Never delete CRM records.

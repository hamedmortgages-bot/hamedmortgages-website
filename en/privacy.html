Privacy Policy

How Hamed Ashouri, Mortgage Broker, collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal information.

Effective date: August 22, 2026 · Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Who we are

This website (hamedmortgages.ca) is operated by Hamed Ashouri, Mortgage Broker, Licence #M22004433, authorized to deal in mortgages on behalf of Sherwood Mortgage Group, Brokerage Licence #12176, licensed in Ontario. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to Hamed Ashouri operating this mortgage brokering practice.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and its tools, why we collect it, who we share it with, and your choices. It follows the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, and your role (for example, buyer, homeowner, self-employed, realtor).
  • Mortgage-related information you choose to provide — for example, your goal or purpose, property type and estimated value, timeline, and general details about your situation entered into forms or the assistant.
  • Assistant (NILI) conversations — the text you type and any voice notes you record, and the text transcript produced from a voice note.
  • Technical and interaction data — a randomly generated session ID and submission ID, the pages you arrive from and land on (referring URL), and campaign/attribution parameters in the link you used (such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content, gclid and fbclid).
  • Server and device data — like most websites, our hosting and font providers automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type.

Please do not enter your Social Insurance Number, passwords, banking credentials, full government identification numbers, or complete financial documents into website forms or the assistant. Those are collected later, securely, when your file is set up.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information through: website inquiry and assessment forms; the "Discover your strategy" questionnaire; the NILI assistant (typed messages and voice notes); the online booking tool; the secure document-upload area; and when you contact us by phone or email.

4. Why we collect it (purposes)

  • To respond to your inquiry and communicate with you.
  • To provide preliminary, general mortgage guidance and information.
  • To arrange consultations and follow up with you.
  • To review, assess and structure mortgage options and, where you proceed, to prepare and submit a mortgage application through the brokerage to lenders.
  • To create and maintain service and client records.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, tax and professional obligations.

We do not use your service inquiry as consent to receive marketing. We will only send you newsletters or promotional messages if you separately opt in (see section 6).

5. Service providers and disclosure

We use trusted third-party service providers to operate this website and our practice. They process your information on our behalf, for the purposes above:

  • Make (Celonis) — connects the website tools to our systems (automation/orchestration).
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates the assistant's replies.
  • Speech-to-text transcription (Whisper) — converts a voice note into text so the assistant can understand it.
  • Zoho — CRM, Forms, Bookings and WorkDrive, used to manage inquiries, appointments, records and document uploads (accessed through Zoho's Canadian (.ca) service endpoints).
  • Google Fonts and GitHub Pages — deliver the website's fonts and pages.

We also disclose your information, as needed and with your knowledge, to our sponsoring brokerage (Sherwood Mortgage Group) and, where you choose to proceed, to lenders, insurers, appraisers, lawyers and other parties required to arrange your mortgage.

Processing outside Canada. Some of these providers (including Make, Anthropic, the transcription provider, Google and GitHub) process and may store information outside Canada, including in the United States. While information is handled by a provider in another country, it may be accessible to the courts, law-enforcement and national-security authorities of that country. You can ask us for more detail about where a specific provider processes data.

6. Consent

By submitting information through our forms or the assistant, you consent to our collecting, using and disclosing that information for the purposes described in this policy. Because mortgage information is sensitive, we rely on your express consent for it, and you can decline to provide information (though this may limit our ability to help you).

Marketing is separate. Consent to receive commercial electronic messages (such as newsletters or promotions) is optional, is never bundled with a service request, and is off by default. Where offered, you must opt in separately, and every such message will identify us and include an unsubscribe option, consistent with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual limits, by contacting our Privacy Officer (section 10).

7. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above and to meet applicable legal, regulatory, tax and professional record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified. Specific retention periods for our systems are being finalized and can be confirmed on request.

8. Safeguards

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information with safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity. To keep your information safe, please do not send sensitive identification or financial documents by email, text or chat — use the secure document-upload area in your Mortgage Workspace instead.

9. Your access and correction rights

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy, and to request corrections. We will respond within the time required by law. To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity first.

10. Privacy Officer and complaints

For any privacy question, request, or to withdraw consent, contact our Privacy Officer:

  • Hamed Ashouri, Mortgage Broker
  • Email: hamedmortgages@gmail.com
  • Phone: (416) 856-8148

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Other

Our website may link to third-party sites (for example, the booking, forms and document-upload tools, and the CMHC rental calculator); their own privacy practices apply once you leave our site. Our services are intended for adults and are not directed at children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the current version, and material changes will be reflected here.

Mortgage Strategy

Structure the decision before choosing the mortgage.

A mortgage is part of a broader financial picture. We examine what you are trying to accomplish, what constraints matter, and which trade-offs are acceptable before selecting a route.

Buying

Budget range, down payment, qualification, offer timing, lender flexibility and future plans should be aligned before you commit to the property.

Refinancing

Refinancing can solve a cash-flow problem, reorganize debt or unlock capital — but only if the economics of the move make sense.

Renewing

A renewal is a strategic checkpoint. Before signing the lender’s offer, review rate, term, prepayment flexibility, debt structure and future plans.

Complex Financing

Business owners, self-employed borrowers, investors and unusual income structures often need lender selection and documentation strategy, not generic rate shopping.
